What if you could pause time... but only you kept getting older?

Monday. Alarm. Click. The whole world stops. You sleep nine more hours. Nobody notices.

You pause to finish your homework. To win every race. To eat every cake in the bakery. To draw a moustache on your teacher.

Test tomorrow? Pause. Study for a week. Top marks.

Late for school? Pause. Stroll in. Early.

It's perfect. For a while.

Then one morning, in the mirror... a grey hair.

You do the maths. The world was frozen. You weren't. Nine hours here, a week there. You're sixteen on paper. Your body is twenty-six.

Your friends laugh at jokes you heard years ago. Your mum asks why you look so tired.

So you stop. No more pausing.

Until a bus runs a red light, straight at your little sister. Click.

Everything freezes. You run out, and carry her to the pavement.

And across the frozen street... someone else is moving. An old man. Grey. Wrinkled. A moustache drawn on his face.

Your teacher.

He's thirty-four. He looks eighty. He points at the moustache.

"I saw that," he says.
