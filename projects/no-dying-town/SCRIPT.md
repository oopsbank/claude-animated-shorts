# The Town Where You're Not Allowed to Die / Thị trấn nơi bạn không được phép chết
Channel: Some Crazy Things. Format: ~55 s vertical 1080x1920, cartoon 3D (bridge look), English voice (ElevenLabs George), animated captions (water-short style).

## Facts and sources
1. Longyearbyen, Svalbard (Norway), one of the northernmost towns on Earth. People leaving the settlement carry protection against polar bears (usually a rifle).
2. The ground is permafrost: it never thaws below the surface.
3. October 1918: young miners died of the Spanish flu and were buried in the small cemetery.
4. Around 1950 it was found that bodies in the cemetery were not decomposing; the cemetery was closed to new burials. Only urns, with permission, since then.
   - https://theculturetrip.com/europe/norway/articles/why-its-illegal-to-die-in-this-town-in-norway
   - https://messynessychic.com/2018/03/26/the-town-where-its-illegal-to-die
5. 1998: an expedition led by Kirsty Duncan exhumed the 1918 miners to look for the flu virus (bodies turned out less preserved than hoped). The 1918 flu killed an estimated ~50 million people.
   - https://www.spokesman.com/stories/1996/may/19/frozen-graves-may-hold-answers-to-epidemic/
   - https://johnsnowsociety.org/2000/09/09/2000-professor-john-oxford-the-search-for-permafrost-and-other-victims-of-the-1918-influenza/
6. It is not actually a law: the seriously ill and very old are sent to mainland Norway (small hospital, no elderly care). Pregnant women go to the mainland about three weeks before the due date.
   - https://scienceinsights.org/why-cant-you-give-birth-in-svalbard/
   - https://www.spitsbergen-svalbard.com/?p=68159

## Director's decisions (retention)
- Hook = the myth itself, stated flat, then "they have a very good reason" so the viewer waits for it.
- No bodies shown, ever: coffins in ice, wooden crosses, cartoon virus characters.
- Honesty beat at 39.5 s ("technically, dying isn't against the law") keeps the comments from "well actually" and earns trust.
- Twist: works both ways (births too).
- Loop: last line ends with the first line.
