What if you woke up tomorrow, and you could hear everyone's thoughts?

Day one is amazing. The barista thinks your new haircut is brave. Your boss is thinking about tacos. During the meeting. Your dog is thinking about one thing. Ball. Ball. Ball.

And the test? Easy. The smart kid behind you is thinking every answer. Really loudly.

Day two, it gets weird. Your best friend thinks your cooking is terrible. Your mom is still thinking about the vase you broke when you were eight. And on the bus, forty strangers are humming the same annoying song. In their heads.

Day three, you can't turn it off. A city of eight million people, all thinking at once. Worries. Grocery lists. That embarrassing thing they said in 2009. You can't sleep. You can't even hear your own thoughts anymore.

So you run. Far away from people. Deep into the woods, where it's finally quiet.

Silence. Peace. Bliss.

Then, a tiny voice. Wait... can you hear us?

It's a squirrel. And behind it, every squirrel in the forest is thinking the exact same thing. Where did I bury my nuts? Where did I bury my nuts?

Turns out, people were the quiet ones.
