# The Lake That Killed 1,700 People in One Night / Hồ nước giết 1.700 người chỉ trong một đêm
Channel: Some Crazy Things. Format: ~55 s vertical, cartoon 3D (bridge look), English voice (ElevenLabs George), animated captions.

## Facts and sources
1. 21 August 1986, Lake Nyos, northwest Cameroon: a limnic eruption released a cloud of CO2 that killed 1,746 people and ~3,500 livestock, up to ~25 km from the lake.
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Nyos_disaster
   - https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/august-21/gas-cloud-kills-cameroon-villagers
2. CO2 from magma below seeps into the deep water; the cold dense bottom layer holds it under pressure (the "shaken soda" picture). Trigger unknown: landslide, small eruption or cold rain are all proposed.
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Nyos
3. CO2 is heavier than air, so the cloud hugged the ground and flowed down the valleys into villages.
4. Survivors report a warm feeling and a rotten-egg smell before passing out; some woke 6-36 hours later. Family members sleeping on the floor died where they lay.
   - USGS Open-File Report 87-97: https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/1987/0097/report.pdf
5. The lake turned from blue to deep red: iron-rich deep water rose and oxidised.
   - https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/lake-nyos-the-deadliest-lake-in-the-world
6. Degassing pipes since 2001 (two more in 2011) let the gas out slowly.
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Nyos

## Director's decisions (retention)
- **Hook = calm lake + huge number.** A pretty, quiet lake at night with "killed 1,700 people" is the contradiction that stops the scroll. "It didn't flood. It didn't boil." stacks two wrong guesses so the viewer starts guessing too.
- **Mystery before mechanism.** The silent morning (no fire, no damage) at ~6 s keeps people waiting for the answer.
- **One clear picture for the science:** the lake is a shaken soda bottle; the cold top water is the cap.
- **Quiet beat at ~30 s, no music, no jokes.** Victims are never shown as characters dying; only empty doorways and still fields. No faces of victims.
- **Crazy detail as the release:** the lake turned red.
- **Loop ending:** the last line "...like it did one night in 1986" runs straight into the first line "One night in 1986...".
