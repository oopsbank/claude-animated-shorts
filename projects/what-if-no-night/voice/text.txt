What if night never came again?
Picture it. The sun climbs to noon... and just stops. Right there. Forever.
Day one feels amazing. Endless summer. Nobody has to go home. Kids play until they fall asleep standing up.
But your body has a clock, and it's waiting for darkness. No dark, no sleep hormone. By day three, everyone is grumpy, clumsy, and seeing things.
Then nature freaks out. Owls can't hunt. Fireflies can't find each other. Flowers that open at night just... don't.
Meanwhile, the sun keeps cooking the same side of the planet. No night means no cooling off. The ground bakes, the lakes shrink, and the air gets hotter every single hour.
Here's the crazy part. On the other side of the Earth, it's the opposite. Endless night. Freezing cold. The ocean slowly turns to ice.
And right between them, on a thin ring of forever-sunset, that's where everyone would move. Billions of people, squeezed into one golden strip of evening.
So the next time you're sad that the day is over, remember: the night isn't stealing your time. It's saving the planet.
Now go to sleep. While you still can.
