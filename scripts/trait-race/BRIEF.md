## What the reference does (structure only; never copy its traits list, challenge, shots, title or look details)
- 4-5 white stickmen, each a **personified trait** with a coloured label and a coloured glow aura (e.g. ego with a crown, discipline with a headband).
- They all start the **same challenge** side by side (there: climbing ropes to the top on a dark background, with a progress %).
- Each one drops out in a way that fits its trait (obsession burns out and tears, ego falls, the "day 1" beginner keeps going); one steady trait wins.
- Dark background, neon glow, big bold "WHO REACHES…" title card, minimal props. The title asks the viewer to guess the winner, which drives comments.

## Our version
- **Cast:** the stickman (`stickman/`, `stickman/DRAWING_RULES.md`), off hold for this format. Each racer has one clear identity prop/accessory + colour + label. Acting by pose: big readable silhouettes, held 0.5-1.5 s, real in-betweens.
- **Look:** flat 2D, dark night background with subtle depth layers, coloured glow auras, readable labels at phone size. A new, simple 2D renderer is acceptable (the 3D plate engine is not required).
- **Our own challenge and our own trait set** (the Screenwriter picks; it must differ from rope climbing and from the reference's exact line-up). The challenge must escalate in 3 stages with a visible progress meter.
- **Voice-over:** one narrator, 225-240 words (62-66 s), plain spoken English, one ElevenLabs pass (~1,250 chars). The narrator calls the race like a deadpan sports commentator; that is where the ABSURD comedy lives (absurd escalations, imagined cutaways framed as fantasy).
