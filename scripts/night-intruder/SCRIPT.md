# Night Intruder (video 13, `night-intruder`, 2026-11-25)

**Written by the Director** (2026-11-25; his Vietnamese original is in `REVIEW_VI.md`). An original short film, no narrator: picture + sound design + one line of dialogue. Length **80 s** (his timeline, 0:00-1:20). Ends on a cut to black with an open ending (viewers guess in the comments). Look: the stickman story style with real perspective sets (HANDOFF › Direction set on 2026-11-25) plus the round-3 style test (night blues, flashlight cones, staircase, prop macros: `flat2d/style_test_rooms3.py`). 30 fps → 2400 frames.

Violence stays cartoon-level: no blood, no visible wounds; impacts sold by sound, sparks and camera shake.

## Characters
- **ME** (the homeowner, 20s, white stickman, pyjamas/T-shirt, messy hair, barefoot). The camera stays close to him (handheld, over-the-shoulder).
- **THIEF 1** (dark hooded jacket, hood up, black face mask, backpack, short crowbar on his hip, flashlight clamped under his chin).
- **THIEF 2** (tall, broad, dark coat; mostly a silhouette against the doorway light; something metal and heavy in his right hand).
- No role labels (film format). Faces: `FACE_STYLE='ref'`, bigger-head proportions; big expressive close-ups.

## Set
A two-storey house at night: upstairs bedroom (bed, nightstand with phone, a heavy metal desk lamp plugged into the wall), a dark hallway, a staircase down, a living room with shelves/cabinets, a tiled floor, a laptop, a front door with a street lamp outside. Night grade: deep blues, the cold white flashlight cone as the main light, warm street-lamp spill only when the door opens.
