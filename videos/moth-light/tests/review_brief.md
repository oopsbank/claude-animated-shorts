# Review brief: moth-light stills (video 11, "Why Do Moths Fly Into Lights?")

House look: flat 2D, white stickmen with a black outline (scientists with red headlamps, watchers), rich layered
backgrounds, big titles, "sentence" captions (white, 1-2 words). Serious, curious tone, no comedy. Quality bar: Blood
Falls / Wind Cave (reference sheet `videos/wind-cave/tests/sheet_2s.jpg`). THE MOTHS ARE THE STARS: they must look like
real hawk moths (feathery antennae, fuzzy thorax, small head with dark eyes, olive-brown patterned forewings with a pale
band and fine lines, warm rose-orange hindwings, thin legs, wing motion blur in fast flight, flight paths as
long-exposure light trails). A yellow BACK arrow on the moth's back shows which side faces the light; in the mechanism a
white UP arrow shows where the moth thinks "up" is. Worlds: a misty night cloud forest lit by a violet UV lamp; the
infrared camera view (grey-green); a night-sky diagram stage.
